[h1]Skills and Stats Progress FIXED[/h1]

Displays detailed skill XP progress, attribute experience, travel distance, speed info, and radiation stats on the duplicant stats panel. A fixed and updated version of Mantakus's SkillsAndStatsProgress mod, rebuilt for the current game version.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Skill XP Progress[/b] - Shows current XP, XP needed for next skillpoint, percentage progress, and available/total skillpoints
[*][b]Attribute Experience[/b] - Displays per-attribute XP progress with level, experience, max XP, and percentage towards next level
[*][b]Active XP Highlighting[/b] - Attributes currently gaining experience are highlighted with bold formatting
[*][b]Travel Distance Tracking[/b] - Shows distance traveled today and total, broken down by navigation type (floor, ladder, tube, etc.)
[*][b]Speed Display[/b] - Real-time movement speed, position coordinates, and configurable average speed calculation
[*][b]Radiation Info[/b] - Radiation balance, change rate per cycle, recovery, current exposure, and resistance (Spaced Out DLC)
[*][b]Workable Efficiency Pop-ups[/b] - Optional floating text showing work efficiency, attribute levels, and XP gain when duplicants work on buildings
[*][b]Delta Tracking[/b] - Optional XP change tracking over configurable time intervals
[/list]

[h2]Configuration[/h2]
All options are configurable via the in-game Mod Options menu. Settings are grouped into:
[list]
[*][b]Display[/b] - Control what's shown: max XP, required XP, stat name truncation, tab switching, XP precision
[*][b]Speed & Travel[/b] - Toggle speed display, average speed interval, travel distance tracking
[*][b]Tracking[/b] - Enable delta tracking with configurable snapshot intervals and sample rates
[*][b]Radiation[/b] - Toggle radiation info panel (Spaced Out DLC only)
[*][b]Workable Info[/b] - Configure efficiency pop-ups: visibility, speed, duration, font size, selected-dupe-only mode
[/list]

[h2]How to Use[/h2]
[olist]
[*][b]Install and enable[/b] - Subscribe and enable in the Mods menu
[*][b]Select a duplicant[/b] - Click on any duplicant to open their details panel
[*][b]View the Stats tab[/b] - The stats panel now shows detailed XP progress, speed, travel, and more
[/olist]

[h3]Tips[/h3]
[list]
[*]Enable "Alter Tab Sort Order" in options to auto-switch to the stats tab when selecting a duplicant
[*]Use "Shrink Stat Name" to save space if the panel is too wide
[*]Enable "Delta Tracking" to see how quickly your duplicants are gaining XP over time
[/list]
