[h1]Controlled Visuals[/h1]

A performance optimization mod that allows you to reduce the visual quality of pipe animations to improve frame rates in large colonies.

[h2]Features[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Configurable Pipe Animation Quality[/b] - Choose between Full, Reduced, or Minimal quality for liquid and gas pipe flow animations
[*][b]Smart Zoom Detection[/b] - Automatically reduces animation updates further when zoomed out
[*][b]No Gameplay Impact[/b] - Only affects visual rendering, not actual pipe mechanics
[/list]

[h2]How to Use[/h2]

[olist]
[*]Enable the mod in your mod list
[*]Access the mod options (gear icon in the mod list)
[*]Select your preferred [b]Pipe Animation Quality[/b]:
[list]
[*][b]Full[/b]: Unchanged from base game (every frame)
[*][b]Reduced[/b]: Updates at 10 FPS - Recommended for most players
[*][b]Minimal[/b]: Updates at 2 FPS - For large colonies
[/list]
[*]Restart the game for changes to take effect
[/olist]

[h2]Performance Impact[/h2]

[b]Medium Performance Improvement[/b]

The performance benefit depends on your colony size and pipe network complexity:
[list]
[*]Small colonies (< 50 dupes): Minimal improvement
[*]Medium colonies (50-100 dupes): Noticeable improvement
[*]Large colonies (100+ dupes with extensive piping): Significant improvement
[/list]
