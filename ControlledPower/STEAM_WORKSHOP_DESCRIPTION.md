[h1]Controlled Power[/h1]

Power diode for ONI: one-way power flow between circuits, with upstream logic/load reporting that includes downstream demand.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]One-way power flow[/b] — Power moves input -> output only.
[*][b]Transformer-style behavior[/b] — Uses stable vanilla transformer mechanics.
[*][b]Upstream current reporting[/b] — Input-side logic/wire readouts include downstream current load.
[*][b]Upstream potential reporting[/b] — Input-side potential includes downstream demand across diode chains.
[/list]

[h2]How to Use[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Research the Power Diode in the tech tree (power control).
[*]Place it between two wire networks (input = source, output = load).
[*]Use upstream wattage sensors to monitor total downstream demand.
[/olist]

[h2]Warning[/h2]
[list]
[*]Do not feed two diode outputs into the same circuit. Upstream power calculations are currently designed for a single diode path and may be incorrect with merged diode outputs.
[/list]
