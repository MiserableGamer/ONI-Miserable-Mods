[h1]Controlled Warnings[/h1]

Adds enhanced alerts for critical situations that can be easily missed in the standard game notifications.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Trapped Duplicant Alerts[/b] - Adds a left-side notification when a dupe gets trapped
[*][b]Critical Escalation[/b] - Alert escalates to critical (red) if a trapped dupe is also suffocating
[*][b]Click to Jump[/b] - Click the notification to center camera on the trapped dupe
[*][b]Pause on Critical[/b] - Optional game pause when a critical alert triggers
[*][b]Configurable Cooldowns[/b] - Prevents alert spam with adjustable cooldown periods
[/list]

[h2]Mod Options[/h2]
[list]
[*]Enable/disable trapped alerts
[*]Persistent or auto-expiring alerts
[*]Enable/disable critical escalation
[*]Pause on critical option
[*]Adjustable alert cooldown
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Suppress Notifications[/b] - Fully compatible
[*][b]DLC Support[/b] - Works with base game and all DLC
[/list]

[h2]Performance[/h2]
Minimal impact - hooks into the existing trapped duplicant diagnostic cycle.
