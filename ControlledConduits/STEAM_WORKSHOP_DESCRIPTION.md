[h1]Controlled Conduits[/h1]

Adds gas, liquid, and conveyor volume sensors that detect packet mass in conduits for automation.

[h2]What it does[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Gas Conduit Volume Sensor[/b] (HVAC) – reads gas packet mass
[*][b]Liquid Conduit Volume Sensor[/b] (Plumbing) – reads liquid packet mass
[*][b]Conveyor Volume Sensor[/b] (Shipping) – reads solid packet mass
[/list]
