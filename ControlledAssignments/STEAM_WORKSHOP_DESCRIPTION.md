[h1]Controlled Assignments[/h1]

Makes showers and sinks assignable to individual duplicants, just like beds and toilets.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Assignable Showers[/b] - Assign specific duplicants to showers, or leave them public for anyone to use
[*][b]Assignable Sinks[/b] - Assign specific duplicants to wash basins, wash sinks, and hand sanitizers
[*][b]Mod Compatible[/b] - Automatically works with modded showers and sinks that use vanilla components
[/list]

[h2]How to Use[/h2]
[olist]
[*][b]Build a shower or sink[/b] - Place any shower or sink building
[*][b]Select the building[/b] - Click on the building to open its details panel
[*][b]Assign a duplicant[/b] - Use the assignment dropdown to assign a specific duplicant, or leave as "Public"
[/olist]

[h3]Tips[/h3]
[list]
[*]Unassigned buildings default to "Public" so all duplicants can use them
[*]Works the same way as bed and toilet assignments
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Oxygen Not Included[/b] - Build 700386 or later
[*][b]Mod API[/b] - Version 2
[*][b]DLC Support[/b] - Works with base game and all DLC (including Bionic Booster Pack)
[*][b]Other Mods[/b] - Compatible with most mods; automatically supports modded showers and sinks
[/list]

[h2]Performance[/h2]
[b]Minimal Performance Impact[/b]
[list]
[*]Only runs when buildings spawn — no per-frame overhead
[*]Uses standard game assignment system
[/list]
